Welcome to Spacephage Night Sky Studio

Spacephage Night Sky Studio is a collection of my astrophotography captured under the dark skies of Flagstaff, Arizona. Here you will find fine-art-prints, 1:1 lessons, and image processing services designed to help others experience and create stunning views of the night sky. Explore the gallery below to begin your journey.

Deep Sky Gallery

Explore a collection of various nebula and galaxies captured with an intermediate telescope setup. Each image highlights the beauty and design of the cosmos through long exposure photography.

Emission Nebulae

Emission Nebulae are vast clouds of glowing gas and dust where stars are born. These vibrant colors reveal intricate shapes and wonderful detail when captured using narrowband filters.

Dark Nebulae

Dark Nebulae are dense clouds of dust that block out starlight behind them, creating silhouettes against the Milky Way. Some can be thick clumps of dust shrouding stars completely, whereas others can be much more diffuse and glow beautifully blue.

Galaxies

Galaxies are immense collections of billions of stars, along with dark lanes of dust, and mysterious dark matter all bound together by gravity. Many harbor supermassive black holes at their centers and hundreds of thousands of light years across.
